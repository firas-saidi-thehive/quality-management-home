Quality FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is The Hive Quality Management System?

The QMS defines how The Hive manages processes, responsibilities, controls, performance, risks, corrective actions, and continuous improvement.

Where can I find approved policies and procedures?

All approved quality policies, procedures, standards, templates, and records are available within the Quality Management Resources library.

How do I report a non-conformity?

Use the Report Quality Issue form to register the issue. It will then be assessed, assigned, investigated, and tracked through resolution.

What is a CAPA?

A Corrective and Preventive Action addresses the root cause of an identified issue and helps prevent recurrence or similar future problems.

Who is responsible for process quality?

Each controlled process has a designated Process Owner responsible for its performance, documentation, controls, risks, and improvement.

Continuous Improvement

Turning Evidence Into Improvement

Quality management at The Hive is not limited to documenting processes. We continuously evaluate how our services and operations perform, identify weaknesses and opportunities, understand root causes, implement corrective actions, and measure whether improvements are effective.

Plan→Do→Check→Improve
1

Report an issue

Identify a deviation, weakness, recurring problem, client concern, or improvement opportunity.

2

Analyze

Determine impact, contributing factors, and root cause.

3

Correct

Define and implement appropriate corrective or preventive actions.

4

Verify

Confirm that actions were completed and produced the expected result.

5

Improve

Apply lessons learned across relevant processes and teams.
