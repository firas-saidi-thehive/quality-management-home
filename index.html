The framework

Quality management at The Hive

The QMS brings together policies, processes, responsibilities, controls, performance indicators, risks, audits, corrective actions, management reviews, and improvement activities in one coordinated framework.

Our objective is not simply to document how we work. The system helps us understand process performance, identify weaknesses early, maintain accountability, manage risk, preserve evidence, learn from experience, and improve the way we deliver services.

Our quality principles

Client focus

Understand requirements and expectations so services deliver measurable value.

Process ownership

Give every critical process a clear owner accountable for effectiveness and improvement.

Evidence-based management

Use KPIs, audits, feedback, incidents, risks, and reviews to guide decisions.

Risk-based thinking

Identify potential problems early, strengthen controls, and act on opportunities.

Accountability

Keep responsibilities, approvals, ownership, and decisions clear and traceable.

Controlled documentation

Maintain current, approved, accessible, and properly archived documentation.

Continuous improvement

Turn experience, feedback, audits, and trends into better ways of working.

Shared responsibility

Who makes quality happen?

Quality is embedded in everyday decisions, delivery, ownership, and learning.

Management

Provides strategic direction, governance, oversight, and resources, ensuring significant risks and priorities receive attention.

Quality Management

Coordinates QMS governance, audits, non-conformities, CAPA, reviews, performance monitoring, documentation, and improvement.

Process Owners

Own the effectiveness and ongoing management of assigned processes.

  • Maintain documentation and controls.
  • Monitor KPIs, risks, and performance.
  • Support audits, actions, and improvement.

Managers

Ensure approved processes and controls are implemented and that employees have the understanding, information, and resources needed to work effectively.

Employees

Follow approved requirements, maintain evidence, report issues and risks, participate in reviews, share lessons, and support corrective actions.
